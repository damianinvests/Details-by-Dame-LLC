The protection step

Coat paint that is ready for protection.

A ceramic coating bonds to a prepared surface and creates hydrophobic behavior that can make routine washing easier. It also enhances the appearance of a properly refined finish.

Coating does not remove swirl marks or conceal defects. If you want the paint corrected, that work comes first; then the coating can protect the result.

What coating can and cannot do

Protection, with realistic expectations.

Choose coating for water behavior, gloss, and easier routine care. It is one layer in a maintenance plan, not an armor shell.

HELPS WITH

Water release

Hydrophobic behavior helps water bead and move from the treated surface.

SUPPORTS

Easier upkeep

Routine washing can be simpler when contaminants release more readily.

DOES NOT

Make paint scratch-proof

Coating does not prevent rock chips, deep scratches, or all paint damage.

Ceramic coating FAQ

Before you book.

Do I need paint correction before ceramic coating?

It depends on the condition and your goals. If visible defects matter to you, correct them before coating so they are not left beneath the protection.

Will ceramic coating prevent scratches?

No. Coating supports water behavior and upkeep but does not make paint immune to scratches, chips, or impact damage.

How do I maintain a coated vehicle?

Follow the care guidance for your coating and use safe wash methods. Mobile maintenance detailing can help keep the finish clean.
