    Foam wash

    Thick foam lifts loose dirt and grit before anything touches the paint, so washing does not add new scratches.

  2. 2Add job photo here
    Step 2: Iron remover and clay
    Step 2 of 6

    Iron remover and clay

    Iron remover dissolves embedded metal particles. A clay bar then pulls off what is bonded to the surface, leaving the paint smooth enough to polish.

  3. 3Add job photo here
    Step 3: Compounding
    Step 3 of 6

    Compounding

    Machine compounding levels the damaged clear coat to cut out swirl marks, scratches and water-spot etching.

  4. 4Add job photo here
    Step 4: Polishing
    Step 4 of 6

    Polishing

    A finer polish refines the surface after compounding and brings back depth, clarity and a sharp reflection.
