THE COMPLETE 8-STAGE DETAILING EXPERIENCE

Scroll to follow your vehicle through every stage of our surgical reconditioning protocol. From the initial high-pressure rinse to the final ceramic flash cure, observe the transformation in real time on the 3D vehicle canvas behind you.

Stage 01 of 08

01. High-Pressure Touchless Pre-Rinse

Before any wash mitt touches the surface, we blast away loose road grit, abrasive silica, and traffic film using calibrated high-pressure deionized water. This step prevents drag marring and establishes a safe foundation for foam lubrication.

✓ Road dust stripped • Surface wet-sheeted • Zero clear coat scratching
Stage 02 of 08

02. Dense Snow Foam Pre-Soak

A thick blanket of pH-neutral citrus snow foam is applied across the entire vehicle body. The foam clings to vertical panels, encapsulating microscopic contaminants, organic grime, and lovebug proteins to safely lift them away from the paint.

✓ Ultra-dense foam barrier • Grime encapsulation • Safe on rubber & trim
Stage 03 of 08

03. Two-Bucket Contact Hand Bath

Using premium ultra-soft microfiber wash mitts and grit-guard filtered buckets, each panel is cleansed with straight-line motions. Separate buckets isolate trapped grit from re-entering the wash solution, eliminating wash-induced swirls.

✓ Dual grit-guard isolation • Linear stroke technique • Zero swirl contamination
Stage 04 of 08

04. Iron Remover Fallout Dissolution & Fine Clay Bar

We apply a specialized pH-balanced chemical iron fallout remover that reacts with embedded brake dust and rail dust, turning purple as it dissolves ferrous metal particles. A synthetic fine-grade clay bar then shears away bonded industrial fallout, leaving the clear coat glass-smooth.

✓ Chemical iron purple bleed • Glass-smooth tactile finish • Sub-surface prep
Stage 05 of 08

05. Touchless Filtered Heated Air Drying

We eliminate towel contact friction by using commercial heated filtered blowers to purge water from mirror seams, emblems, grilles, and lug nuts, finished with 1,200 GSM plush microfiber towels to prevent water spots and mineral streaks.

✓ Crevice water purging • Heated air dry • 100% moisture elimination
Stage 06 of 08

06. Precision Dual-Action Machine Compounding & Jeweling Polish

Each panel is measured in microns with digital ultrasonic paint depth gauges. We then execute a multi-stage compound and finishing polish under high-CRI inspection lights, safely leveling micro-scratches, wash swirls, and etching to unlock mirror reflections.

✓ 85%–95%+ Defect removal • Clear coat depth preserved • Showroom gloss unlocked
Stage 07 of 08

07. Isopropyl Alcohol (IPA) Solvent Panel Wipe

To ensure permanent ceramic bonding, all compounding lubricants, polishing oils, and fillers are stripped using pure solvent-grade surface prep solutions. This reveals true, naked clear coat with zero artificial fillers.

✓ Zero silicone fillers • Chemically clean clear coat • Maximum bond adhesion
Stage 08 of 08

08. 9H Nano-Ceramic Coating Installation & IR Flash Cure

Concentrated SiO2 nano-particles are applied panel-by-panel. As the carrier solvent flashes with a characteristic rainbow oil slick, the coating is leveled with low-lint suede towels. The cured ceramic layer creates an ultra-hydrophobic, UV-resistant glass shield.

✓ 1 to 5-Year Certified Warranty • Contact angle > 110° • Extreme candy gloss

Oviedo, FL Project

MERCEDES-BENZ E-CLASS: HARD WATER ETCHING RESTORATION

Vehicle: Mercedes-Benz E-Class | Location: Oviedo, FL | Service: Two-Stage Paint Correction + 3-Year Ceramic Coating

The Challenge

The client's black Mercedes-Benz was parked adjacent to a lawn sprinkler zone utilizing untreated well water in Oviedo, FL. Calcium, magnesium, and sulfur deposits had etched into the clear coat, creating stubborn ring marks that standard car washes could not budge. Additionally, micro-marring covered 80% of the horizontal panels.

The Solution

Damian executed chemical mineral dissolution followed by ultrasonic paint thickness gauging. Using dual-action polishers paired with micro-wool cutting pads and diminishing abrasives, the etched craters were safely leveled without compromising clear coat integrity. A finishing polish with ultra-soft foam brought the obsidian paint to optical clarity before locking it in with a 3-Year 9H ceramic coating.

Final Outcome: 95% defect removal, mirror gloss restored, permanent water-spot defense.
