The 6-Step Paint Correction Process

Why our results exceed ordinary mobile car washes: We combine 10+ years of technical training, precise clear coat readings, and tested compounding chemistry.

01
1

Chemical & Mechanical Decon

We begin with a high-lubricity pH-neutral foam wash, followed by iron fall-out chemical removers and synthetic clay bars to pull embedded brake dust and rail dust from deep pores.

02
2

Digital Micron Paint Gauge Readings

We map paint thickness across all panels (hood, fenders, quarter panels) in micrometers to confirm adequate clear coat remains to safely correct defects without strike-through risk.

03
3

Dedicated Test Spot Calibration

Every automotive brand has distinct clear coat hardness (e.g., sticky soft Porsche/Subaru paint vs rock-hard Mercedes/Corvette clear). We test pad and compound pairings for the optimal formula.

04
4

Multi-Stage Compounding & Polish

Stage 1 cuts through heavy swirls, etching, and scratches with micro-abrasive compounding. Stage 2 refines with a jewel-polishing foam pad to remove micro-marring and unleash high-def gloss.

05
5

Isopropanol (IPA) Surface Wipe

We wipe down the entire body with specialized alcohol panel cleansers. This strips away all polishing lubricants and carrier oils to reveal true, honest correction under 2,000-lumen lighting.

06
6

Ceramic Armor Shield Seal

With the clear coat fully leveled and surgically clean, we lock in the finish using a professional 3, 5, or 7-year nano-ceramic coating, ensuring years of hydrophobic, scratch-resistant protection.

Frequently Asked Questions

Clear answers about paint correction, ceramic coatings, and mobile scheduling.

Standard detailing cleans and dresses surfaces, while generic dealership "buffing" often glazes paint with cheap fillers that wash away in two weeks. Paint correction is a mechanical leveling process using precision abrasives and dual-action machines to permanently remove clear coat defects, swirl marks, scratches, and haze. The defects are gone for good, leaving a true mirror finish.
Ceramic coatings form a hardened, semi-permanent glass shell over your vehicle. If you apply a coating over swirls, water spots, or scratches, you permanently trap and seal those defects beneath the glass layer for years. Proper paint correction guarantees your car is at its absolute peak visual clarity before locking it in.
