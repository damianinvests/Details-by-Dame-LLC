HOW TO TELL IF A SCRATCH IS TOO DEEP FOR PAINT CORRECTION

Perform the verified Fingernail Test: Run your clean fingernail gently across the scratch perpendicular to the mark. If your fingernail catches, the defect has breached the clear coat layer and entered the base color or primer coat. These deep scratches cannot be polished away without wet sanding or repainting. However, if your nail slides smoothly over the scratch, our multi-stage compound can safely level and erase it completely.

WHY WAX FAILS IN CENTRAL FLORIDA (CERAMIC COATING VS. WAX)

Traditional carnauba wax melts at temperatures between 160°F and 180°F. In Orlando summers, a vehicle’s dark paint surface frequently exceeds 190°F under direct sunlight. As a result, standard waxes liquefy and wash away in less than 30 days. Ceramic coatings resist heat up to 1,000°F+, creating an impenetrable barrier against Florida’s notorious lovebug acidity, bird droppings, and intense UV clear coat oxidation.
