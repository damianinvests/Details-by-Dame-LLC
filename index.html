Paint Correction in East Orlando

Permanent clear-coat leveling. Removing scratches without risking clear-coat burn-through.

Schedule Paint Assessment

In Central Florida, clear coats take punishment from 95°F summer sun, acid rain, lovebug splatter along the 408 and 417, and hard water sprinkler mineral deposits. When you take your car to high-volume car washes or use uncalibrated buffers, millions of circular spider-web scratches form in your clear coat, scattering light and turning deep black, red, or metallic paint into a cloudy gray haze.

At Details by Dame, paint correction is not an "express buff." Backed by 12 years of professional detailing education, Damian inspects every panel with digital ultrasonic paint depth gauges to calculate clear coat safety margins. Using precision dual-action polishers (Rupes, Flex) and micro-abrasive formulations, we level microscopic clear coat ridges down to a flat, mirror baseline.
