Oviedo, FL Project

MERCEDES-BENZ E-CLASS: HARD WATER ETCHING RESTORATION

Vehicle: Mercedes-Benz E-Class | Location: Oviedo, FL | Service: Two-Stage Paint Correction + 3-Year Ceramic Coating

The Challenge

The client's black Mercedes-Benz was parked adjacent to a lawn sprinkler zone utilizing untreated well water in Oviedo, FL. Calcium, magnesium, and sulfur deposits had etched into the clear coat, creating stubborn ring marks that standard car washes could not budge. Additionally, micro-marring covered 80% of the horizontal panels.

The Solution

Damian executed chemical mineral dissolution followed by ultrasonic paint thickness gauging. Using dual-action polishers paired with micro-wool cutting pads and diminishing abrasives, the etched craters were safely leveled without compromising clear coat integrity. A finishing polish with ultra-soft foam brought the obsidian paint to optical clarity before locking it in with a 3-Year 9H ceramic coating.

Final Outcome: 95% defect removal, mirror gloss restored, permanent water-spot defense.

PORSCHE PANAMERA: OBSIDIAN BLACK SWIRL CORRECTION

Vehicle: Porsche Panamera | Location: Waterford Lakes, FL | Service: Multi-Stage Paint Correction + 5-Year Ceramic

The Challenge

Frequent visits to automated tunnel car washes with rotating abrasive brushes had left this Porsche covered in dense spider-web swirls, deep linear gouges, and a gray hazy appearance under direct sunlight.

The Solution

Damian conducted a comprehensive 8-stage decon bath, followed by test spot evaluation on the soft German clear coat. Final Outcome: Deep obsidian mirror reflection, 99.4 GU clarity, zero wash swirls.
