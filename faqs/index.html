Good questions before you book.

A few practical answers about paint correction, ceramic coating, and mobile detailing.

Should paint correction come before ceramic coating?

If visible defects are something you want improved, correction should happen before coating. A coating does not hide existing swirls or scratches.

Does ceramic coating make paint scratch-proof?

No. Ceramic coating can improve water behavior and make maintenance easier, but it does not prevent rock chips, deep scratches, or all paint damage.

Can paint correction remove every scratch?

No. Some light defects may improve with polishing, while deeper scratches may be too severe for safe correction. The paint should be assessed before promising a result.

Can you come to my home or workplace?

Mobile detailing is offered in listed service areas. Contact Details by Dame with your address to confirm availability.

What should I include in a quote request?

Include your vehicle year, make, model, the service you are considering, and what you would like improved. Photos can help describe the condition.
