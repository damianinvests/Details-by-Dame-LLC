Paint correction

Understanding the finish.

Correction is a measured process. The right result depends on the paint and the defects present.

What does paint correction improve?

Machine polishing can improve the appearance of swirl marks, light scratches, haze, and some water spotting. The achievable result depends on defect depth, paint thickness, and condition.

Can every scratch be removed?

No. Deep scratches, chips, and defects through the clear coat may not be safely removable through polishing. We assess the finish before recommending a process.

Should paint correction come before ceramic coating?

If you want visible defects improved, correction and preparation should happen before coating. A coating protects the prepared surface; it does not hide existing swirls or scratches.

Does ceramic coating make paint scratch-proof?

No. Coatings can improve water behavior and make maintenance easier, but they do not prevent rock chips, deep scratches, or all paint damage.

How long does a ceramic coating last?

Durability depends on the product, preparation, application, driving conditions, washing habits, and maintenance. We discuss realistic expectations for the specific coating selected.
