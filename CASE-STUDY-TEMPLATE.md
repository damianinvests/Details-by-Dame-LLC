# Details by Dame — Case Study Publishing Template

Use this structure for every new vehicle project page. Keep the same customer journey and three-service explanation while making the vehicle condition, correction plan, coating, location, and result specific to the real job.

## H1 format
`[Year, if relevant] [Make] [Model] [Trim, if relevant] Paint Correction and Ceramic Coating in [City], Florida`

Examples:
- `2023 Corvette C8.R Edition Paint Correction and Ceramic Coating in Melbourne, Florida`
- `Mercedes-Benz E350 Paint Correction and Ceramic Coating in Oviedo, Florida`

Use the actual city where the work was completed. Do not infer or reuse a city from an old URL or page title. Update the page title, meta description, Open Graph title/description, visible location, image alt text, and WebPage JSON-LD to match the new case study.

## Required story sequence
1. **Project overview:** year/make/model/trim, color if known, actual city, observed paint condition, correction level, coating duration, and goal.
2. **Customer journey:** quote request → consultation and in-person paint inspection → lighting assessment → test spot → explain one-step/two-step/multi-stage options → agree on a package that matches vehicle needs and customer budget → schedule.
3. **Wash and decontamination:** pre-rinse/foam as appropriate, safe contact wash, wheels/tires and exterior details within scope, iron-remover treatment, and clay-bar decontamination when needed.
4. **Paint assessment:** paint-thickness-gauge readings where accessible, panel-by-panel inspection, test spot results, and clear-coat preservation. Never invent numerical readings.
5. **Vehicle-specific correction:** describe actual defects, selected correction stages, polishing approach, and realistic limitations. Do not promise full removal of defects that may be too deep.
6. **Panel preparation:** explain the IPA/panel wipe used to remove polishing oils and residue before coating.
7. **Ceramic coating:** state only the actual coating duration/product claims supplied for that job; describe application, leveling, cure, final inspection, and maintenance. Do not call paint scratch-proof or chip-proof.
8. **Three core services:** separate sections for mobile detailing, paint correction, and ceramic coating. Explain each service's role and link each to:
   - `/mobile-detailing-orlando/`
   - `/paint-correction-orlando/`
   - `/ceramic-coating-orlando/`
9. **CTA:** invite the visitor to request a consultation/quote at `/contact/`.

## Accuracy rules
- Do not imply interior detailing, engine detailing, or any add-on was performed unless confirmed in the job notes.
- Explain mobile detailing as an available on-site cleaning/maintenance service when it was not part of the documented correction/coating scope.
- Do not invent paint thickness values, correction percentages, product brands, cure times, warranties, or customer quotes.
- Make clear that ceramic coating protects a prepared finish but does not remove defects, prevent rock chips, or make paint scratch-proof.
- Keep the page useful and specific: prioritize what was found, why the correction level was chosen, what preparation was done, and what protection was applied.
