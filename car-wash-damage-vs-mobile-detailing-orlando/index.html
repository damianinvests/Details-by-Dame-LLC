Automatic car washes and paint care

The wash method matters. Here is what Orlando drivers should know about swirl marks and routine maintenance.

Automatic car washes are convenient, but any wash method can contribute to surface marring if dirt is dragged across paint or wash materials are not kept clean. The risk depends on the equipment, maintenance, wash process, and condition of the vehicle.

Why swirl marks appear

Fine circular marks often become visible when light reflects across the paint. They can build up through repeated contact washing with contaminated mitts, brushes, towels, or poor technique. A careful hand wash can reduce avoidable contact damage, but no wash method is completely risk-free.

What mobile detailing changes

A professional maintenance detail can provide more control over wash materials and technique, along with interior care. It does not automatically correct existing paint defects. If swirls are already present, paint correction may be the appropriate separate service.
