2023 Corvette C8.R Paint Correction & Ceramic Coating in Melbourne, FL

Melbourne, Florida · One-step paint correction · Five-year ceramic coating

Project overview

  • Vehicle: 2023 Corvette C8.R Edition
  • Project location: Melbourne, Florida
  • Paint condition: Dealership-installed swirl marks on a brand-new vehicle with approximately 1,500 miles
  • Correction: One-step paint correction
  • Protection: Five-year ceramic coating
  • Primary goal: Improve the paint safely, restore gloss and clarity, and protect the corrected finish

From first inquiry to a scheduled detail

Every project starts when the customer reaches out for a quote and tells us about the vehicle, its condition, and the finish they want. We arrange a consultation and paint inspection so we can evaluate the actual vehicle rather than guess from a few photos. During the inspection, we review the paint in suitable lighting, discuss the visible defects and the customer's priorities, and explain realistic outcomes.

We then perform a test spot using an appropriate pad, polish, and machine combination. The test spot helps show what the specific paint responds to and whether the vehicle calls for a one-step, two-step, or more involved multi-stage correction. We also assess paint thickness across panels with a paint thickness gauge where accessible, looking for variations or signs that call for extra caution. Gauge readings are used as a risk-assessment tool, not as a promise that every defect can be removed.

Once the condition and test spot are understood, we walk through the recommended package, the expected improvement, the limitations, and the investment. We agree on a package that fits what the vehicle needs and what the customer is comfortable spending, then schedule the detail.

Arrival, wash, and exterior preparation

On the scheduled day, the vehicle is set up in a suitable work area and receives a careful exterior wash to remove loose dirt and road film. Depending on the contamination present, preparation includes a pre-rinse or foam pre-wash, a careful contact wash using clean wash media, and thorough rinsing and drying. Wheels, tires, jambs, and exterior trim are addressed as part of the agreed exterior-detail scope.

Next comes chemical decontamination. An iron-remover treatment helps dissolve embedded ferrous particles that ordinary washing will not remove. The paint is then assessed and, where needed, mechanically decontaminated with a clay bar and appropriate lubricant to lift bonded contaminants. The goal is a clean, smooth surface before machine polishing—not to use aggressive clay action as a substitute for correction.

Paint correction: the work this vehicle needed

This Corvette came to us brand new from the dealership with approximately 1,500 miles on the odometer, yet the paint already showed dealership-installed swirl marks. A new-car delivery does not guarantee defect-free paint: dealership washing, transport handling, and routine prep can leave visible marring. The objective was to remove the correctable defects with a measured one-step polish, bring out the gloss, and protect the refined finish for the long term.

After inspection and a test spot, the paint condition called for a one-step correction rather than an unnecessarily aggressive multi-stage process. A suitable polishing combination was used to refine the finish and remove the identified dealership swirl marks while respecting the clear coat. The vehicle was then checked under lighting to confirm the improvement before coating preparation.

Correction is performed in controlled sections. We clean pads as needed, inspect the finish under appropriate lighting, and adjust the process if a panel responds differently. Paint thickness and the condition of the clear coat guide how far we can responsibly go. Some deep scratches, etching, or defects that have penetrated too far may need to remain rather than risk compromising the paint.

Panel preparation and ceramic coating

Following correction, the paint was panel-wiped with an IPA-based preparation solution to remove polishing residue and oils. A five-year ceramic coating was then applied to the prepared finish. The coating enhances gloss and water behavior and helps protect against environmental contamination when properly maintained. It is not scratch-proof and does not prevent rock chips or impact damage.

Coating is applied to a properly prepared surface, panel by panel, following the product's application and leveling requirements. We monitor for high spots and missed areas, allow the coating to cure as directed, and complete a final inspection under suitable lighting. The customer receives care guidance so the finish can be maintained correctly.

How our three core services fit together

01 · Mobile detailing

Mobile detailing is the convenient on-site cleaning and care side of our business. Depending on the package selected, it can include interior cleaning, vacuuming, surface care, glass, wheels, tires, and a thorough exterior wash. It is also the maintenance service that helps keep a corrected and coated vehicle looking its best between major paint services. Interior detailing is not represented as completed on this vehicle unless it was included in the agreed scope.

Explore mobile detailing ↗

02 · Paint correction

Paint correction is the machine-polishing process used to improve defects such as wash-induced swirls, marring, and certain scratches. The appropriate level—one-step, two-step, or multi-stage—is determined by inspection and a test spot, not by a one-size-fits-all promise. This is the stage that improves the paint before a protective coating is installed.

Explore paint correction ↗

03 · Ceramic coating

Ceramic coating is the protection stage applied after the paint has been properly cleaned, corrected as agreed, and panel-prepped. It enhances gloss and water behavior and helps with routine maintenance. It does not make paint scratch-proof, stop stone chips, or replace safe washing and maintenance.

Explore ceramic coating ↗

Final result and ongoing care

The finished vehicle leaves with paint that has been refined to the agreed level and protected with the coating selected for this project. The best long-term results come from maintenance washes with safe methods, avoiding automatic brush washes, and following the coating-care guidance. Actual durability varies with driving conditions, storage, washing habits, and maintenance.
