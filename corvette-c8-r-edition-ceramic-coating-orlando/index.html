This Orlando project features a Corvette C8.R Edition. The project gallery documents the vehicle as part of a paint correction and ceramic coating service.

Preparing paint for a coating

A coating is not a substitute for paint correction. If visible swirls or other defects need attention, they should be assessed and addressed before protection is applied. Ceramic coating can then help water release from the treated surface and support routine maintenance.

Coatings do not make paint scratch-proof or prevent impact damage such as rock chips.
