Details by Dame · Paint correction & ceramic coating case study

Tesla Model X Paint Correction and Ceramic Coating in Orlando, Florida

Orlando, Florida · One-step paint correction · Five-year ceramic coating

Project overview

  • Vehicle: Tesla Model X
  • Project location: Orlando, Florida
  • Paint condition: Severe water spots, minor to moderate swirl marks, and scratches
  • Correction: One-step paint correction
  • Protection: Five-year ceramic coating
  • Goal: Improve the paint safely, restore gloss and clarity, and protect the prepared finish

From first inquiry to a scheduled detail

Every project begins when the customer reaches out for a quote and shares the vehicle, location, concerns, and desired result. We arrange a consultation and in-person paint inspection rather than selecting a correction package from assumptions or photos alone. We examine the finish under suitable lighting, discuss the visible defects, and explain what can realistically be improved.

We then perform a test spot with an appropriate machine, pad, and polish combination. The test spot shows how this specific paint responds and helps determine whether it calls for a one-step, two-step, or more involved multi-stage correction. We also use a paint-thickness gauge where the panel and substrate allow, looking for variations and potential risk areas. Gauge readings inform the process; they are not a guarantee that defects can safely be removed.

Once the test spot and inspection are complete, we explain the recommended package, expected improvement, limitations, and investment. We agree on a package that matches the vehicle's needs and the customer's budget, then schedule the detail.

Vehicle arrival, wash, and exterior preparation

On the scheduled day, we begin with a careful exterior wash to remove loose dirt and road film. Depending on the vehicle's condition, preparation may include a pre-rinse or foam pre-wash, a safe contact wash with clean wash media, and thorough rinsing and drying. Wheels, tires, exterior glass, and accessible jambs are addressed within the agreed scope.

We then chemically decontaminate the paint with an iron remover to target embedded ferrous particles that a normal wash cannot remove. Where bonded contamination remains, we use a lubricated clay bar carefully to lift it from the surface. Decontamination creates a cleaner starting point for polishing; it is not a replacement for paint correction.

What this vehicle needed

The Tesla Model X had severe water spotting along with minor to moderate swirl marks and scratches. The water spots were the main concern: mineral deposits can leave visible marks or etching when water is allowed to dry on paint, especially in Florida's heat. The goal was to improve the correctable defects with a one-step correction and apply a highly hydrophobic five-year coating to help water release more readily and make maintenance easier.

After the test spot, a one-step correction was selected to refine the paint, reduce the minor to moderate swirls and scratches, and improve gloss. Severe water spots require careful evaluation: some deposits can be removed during chemical decontamination, while etched marks may need polishing. The one-step process was selected for this vehicle; any deeper etching that could not be safely corrected was not chased at the expense of the clear coat.

Correction is performed in controlled sections and checked under appropriate lighting. We clean pads as needed and adjust the approach if a panel responds differently. Paint thickness and clear-coat condition guide how far we can responsibly go. Deep scratches, severe etching, or defects that have penetrated too far may need to remain rather than risk compromising the finish.

Panel preparation and ceramic coating

After polishing, we use an IPA-based panel wipe or suitable coating-preparation solution to remove polishing oils and residue and reveal the true finish. The prepared paint received a five-year ceramic coating selected for its strong hydrophobic behavior. Its water-repellent surface helps water bead and release more readily, which can reduce how long water remains on the paint and make prompt drying easier. This can help reduce the opportunity for future water spotting, but it cannot guarantee water spots will never form: mineral-rich water can still leave deposits if it dries on the surface. Prompt drying and proper maintenance remain important.

The coating is applied to the properly prepared surface, panel by panel, following the product's application and leveling requirements. We inspect for high spots and missed areas, allow the coating to cure as directed, and complete a final inspection under suitable lighting. The customer receives maintenance guidance for the coating.

How our three core services fit together

01 · Mobile detailing

Mobile detailing brings convenient interior and exterior vehicle care to a suitable home or workplace location. Depending on the package, it can include vacuuming, interior surface cleaning, glass, wheels, tires, and a careful exterior wash. It is also how customers maintain a corrected and coated vehicle between major paint services. We do not claim interior detailing was performed on this particular project unless it was included in the agreed scope.

02 · Paint correction

Paint correction uses machine polishing to improve wash-induced swirl marks, marring, haze, and suitable scratches. The correction level is selected through inspection and a test spot. One-step, two-step, and multi-stage approaches are not interchangeable; the right choice depends on the paint and the customer's priorities.

Explore paint correction ↗

03 · Ceramic coating

Ceramic coating is applied after washing, decontamination, correction as agreed, and panel preparation. It enhances gloss and hydrophobic behavior and can make routine maintenance easier. It does not make paint scratch-proof, prevent rock chips, or eliminate water spots, so safe washing and ongoing care remain important.

Final inspection and ongoing care

We finish by checking the paint and coating under suitable lighting and reviewing care instructions with the customer. Safe maintenance washing, prompt removal of contaminants, and avoiding abrasive automatic brush washes help preserve the finish. Coating durability varies with driving conditions, storage, wash habits, and maintenance.
