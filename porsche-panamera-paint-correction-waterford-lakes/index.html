Black Porsche Panamera

Waterford Lakes, Florida · Black Porsche Panamera · Multi-stage paint correction and ceramic coating

Project overview

  • Vehicle: Porsche Panamera
  • Color: Black
  • Location: Waterford Lakes, Florida
  • Services: Multi-stage paint correction and ceramic coating
  • Recorded condition: Moderate swirl marks
  • Goal: Improve reflections and protect the corrected finish

The challenge

The original project story describes a black Panamera with moderate swirl marks that softened the depth of the paint and scattered reflections in direct sunlight. The owner wanted more than a routine clean: the goal was to improve the finish and protect the result.

Black paint makes fine surface defects easier to notice. The correction plan therefore focused on inspection, appropriate preparation, and controlled polishing rather than assuming every mark could be removed.

Wash and decontamination

The vehicle was thoroughly washed to remove loose dirt and road grime. Chemical decontamination was then used to address embedded contaminants before polishing. The paint was prepared so the correction process could be performed on a clean surface.

Multi-stage paint correction

A multi-stage polishing process was used to improve the visible swirl marks and refine the finish. Each section was worked and inspected with attention to the limits of the clear coat. The aim was sharper reflections and greater clarity without unnecessary removal of paint.

Ceramic coating and final inspection

After the paint reached the agreed finish, a ceramic coating was applied to the prepared surface. The final inspection reviewed the paint under suitable lighting and checked the coating application. The coating supports water behavior and routine maintenance; it does not remove scratches or make paint immune to damage.

Why the order matters

Ceramic coating protects the condition of the paint beneath it. If swirls remain before installation, they can remain visible afterward. That is why correction is considered first when the owner wants those defects improved and the paint can safely be polished.
