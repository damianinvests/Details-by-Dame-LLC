Black Porsche Panamera

Waterford Lakes · Paint correction and ceramic coating project

This Waterford Lakes project involved a black Porsche Panamera with moderate swirl marks that softened the depth of the paint and scattered reflections in direct light. The goal was to refine the finish and protect the corrected surface.

Project overview

  • Vehicle: Black Porsche Panamera
  • Location: Waterford Lakes, Florida
  • Service: Multi-stage paint correction and ceramic coating
  • Observed condition: Moderate swirl marks

How the finish was approached

The project began with a thorough wash and chemical decontamination to prepare the paint for polishing. A multi-stage correction process was then used to improve the visible swirl marks and refine the reflections, with attention to the limits of the vehicle’s clear coat.

After correction, a ceramic coating was applied to the prepared surface. The final inspection checked the finish under appropriate lighting and reviewed the coating application.

Why correction comes before coating

Ceramic coating does not remove swirl marks or scratches. It protects the condition of the paint beneath it, which is why defects that matter to the owner should be assessed and corrected before coating is applied. Actual improvement depends on paint condition and the depth of each defect.
