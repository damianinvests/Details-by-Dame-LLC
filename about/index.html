About Details by Dame

A finish-first detailing approach, built on experience, education, and honest recommendations.

The person behind the work

Experience behind every pass.

Details by Dame, LLC is based in Union Park and serves Orlando and nearby Central Florida communities. Owner Damian brings more than ten years of experience in the automotive detailing industry. Details by Dame is also backed by more than 50 five-star Google reviews from customers.

Before starting Details by Dame, Damian managed local Detail Garage stores in Orlando and Melbourne, with responsibilities that included store operations, team leadership, product education, and training local detailers. Through the Chemical Guys and Detail Garage environment, he learned from industry trainers Chad Zani and Sheldon Moodley. That experience shaped a practical approach to paint correction, polishing, ceramic coating, and vehicle care.

Today, the focus is straightforward: understand the condition of the vehicle, explain what can realistically be improved, and recommend work that fits the owner's goals. Read customer reviews.

How we work

Restore. Protect. Maintain.

The order matters. Each step has a purpose, and not every vehicle needs every service.

01 / ASSESS

Understand the finish

Review the paint and vehicle condition before choosing a process. Set realistic expectations about defects and improvement.

02 / REFINE

Correct with care

Use an appropriate polishing approach to improve clarity while avoiding unnecessary removal of healthy clear coat.

03 / PRESERVE

Protect and maintain

Apply protection when it makes sense, then use appropriate maintenance to preserve the result.
