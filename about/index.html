Careful work. Clear expectations.

A finish-first detailing approach based in Union Park, serving Orlando and nearby Central Florida communities.

Details by Dame, LLC is based in the Union Park area of Orlando, Florida. The business focuses on improving and maintaining vehicle finishes through paint correction, ceramic coating, and mobile detailing.

Owner Damian brings more than ten years of experience in the detailing industry, including local Detail Garage leadership and training experience. The goal is straightforward: assess the vehicle honestly, explain what the work can realistically improve, and recommend only the service that fits the condition and the owner’s priorities.

How we approach the work

01

Assess first

Every finish is different. We review its condition before recommending correction or protection.

02

Set realistic expectations

Not every scratch or mark can be safely polished away. We explain the likely outcome before work begins.

03

Maintain the result

Safe washing and regular maintenance help preserve the appearance of a corrected or coated vehicle.
