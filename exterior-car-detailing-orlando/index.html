Exterior car detailing in Orlando

Routine exterior care keeps the vehicle clean; correction and coating address different paint needs.

Exterior detailing focuses on safely removing everyday dirt and road film while maintaining the appearance of paint, wheels, glass, and trim. The service selected should match the vehicle’s condition and the result you want.

Cleaning versus correction

A wash removes surface contamination but does not remove swirl marks or scratches in the clear coat. If the paint looks hazy or marked after cleaning, a separate paint correction assessment may be appropriate.

Protection

Ceramic coating can add hydrophobic behavior to a prepared surface and support easier maintenance. It does not make paint scratch-proof.
