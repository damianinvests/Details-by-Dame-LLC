Black Porsche Cayman S

Before-and-after paint correction · Central Florida

What the before-and-after shows

The project image shows a black Porsche Cayman S before and after paint correction. Dark paint makes fine wash marks and uneven reflections especially easy to see, so careful inspection and controlled polishing are important.

A measured approach to paint correction

Every vehicle should be assessed before polishing. The process typically includes safe washing, paint decontamination where needed, a test spot, and a polishing approach selected for the paint’s condition. The goal is to improve clarity while avoiding unnecessary removal of healthy clear coat.

Not every scratch can be safely removed. Deep defects may remain after polishing, and the realistic result depends on paint thickness, condition, and the defect itself.
