Privacy Policy

Last updated November 5, 2026.

Information we collect

If you submit a quote request, we collect the information you choose to provide, which may include your name, email address, phone number, vehicle year/make/model, requested service, and details about the vehicle or work you are considering. You may also contact us directly by phone, text, or email.

How we use it

We use inquiry information to respond to your questions, prepare estimates, discuss scheduling, provide requested services, and maintain appropriate business records. Please do not submit payment card numbers, passwords, or other sensitive financial information through this website.

Form delivery provider

The website quote form is delivered through FormSubmit. When you submit it, the information in the form is transmitted to FormSubmit so it can process the submission and forward it to Details by Dame at info@detailsbydame.com. FormSubmit may apply spam-prevention measures and temporarily retain submissions under its own service terms. Review the FormSubmit privacy policy and FormSubmit documentation for details about its handling and retention practices.

Sharing and retention

We do not sell personal information submitted through this website. We may share it with service providers only when reasonably needed to operate the website or respond to your request, or when disclosure is required by law. We retain inquiries for as long as reasonably needed to respond, provide services, and maintain business records. FormSubmit has its own separate retention practices.

Cookies and third-party links

This website does not require an account or accept payments. Links to third-party sites, including Google and FormSubmit, are governed by those providers’ own privacy practices. Their sites may use cookies or other technologies when you visit them.

Your choices and questions

You can choose not to submit the quote form and instead contact Details by Dame directly. For a question about information you submitted to the business, email info@detailsbydame.com or call/text (321) 419-5434.

This notice is a general description of the website’s current practices and is not legal advice. It should be reviewed if the site’s tools or data practices change.
