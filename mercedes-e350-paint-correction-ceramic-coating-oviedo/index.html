Mercedes-Benz E350

Oviedo, Florida · Paint correction and ceramic coating project

This project documents a Mercedes-Benz E350 in Oviedo, Florida. The work is presented as a paint correction and ceramic coating project, showing the vehicle and finish in the supplied project photography.

Why correction comes first

Paint correction is used to refine suitable defects in the clear coat. Ceramic coating is applied after preparation, so existing marks are not simply sealed beneath the coating.

Every vehicle’s paint condition is different. The appropriate level of correction depends on inspection, the defects present, and the owner’s expectations.
