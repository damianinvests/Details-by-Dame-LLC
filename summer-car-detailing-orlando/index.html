Summer car care in Orlando

A few consistent habits can help keep your vehicle cleaner through Florida’s hot, rainy months.

Summer in Central Florida brings strong sun, frequent rain, heat, and periods of heavy pollen or road grime. A sensible maintenance routine helps keep the vehicle presentable and makes it easier to spot issues early.

Simple habits

  • Wash when the surface is cool and avoid letting soap or water dry on hot paint.
  • Use clean wash media and separate tools for wheels and paint.
  • Dry carefully to reduce water spotting.
  • Remove bird droppings and other contaminants promptly using paint-safe methods.
  • Park in shade or covered parking when practical.
  • Maintain any coating or protection according to its care instructions.

When routine washing no longer restores the appearance, an inspection can help determine whether bonded contamination, water-spot treatment, or paint correction is appropriate.
