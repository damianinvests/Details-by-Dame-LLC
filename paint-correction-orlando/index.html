The first step in the finish path

Correct the surface before you protect it.

Paint correction uses machine polishing to refine the clear coat and improve how light reflects from the finish. It is the service to consider when swirl marks, light scratches, water spots, oxidation, or haze make the paint look dull.

Every vehicle is different. We start with the condition of the finish and discuss the improvement that is realistic for your paint before work begins.

01 / ASSESS

Inspect the finish

Review the visible defects and the paint condition before choosing a level of correction.

02 / REFINE

Polish with care

Use a focused polishing approach to improve clarity and gloss without unnecessary removal of healthy clear coat.

03 / PROTECT

Choose what follows

After correction, consider ceramic coating to protect the refined surface or mobile detailing to maintain it.

Set the right expectation

Improve what polishing can safely reach.

Paint correction is a finish-refinement service, not body repair. An inspection helps establish what can be improved safely.

OFTEN IMPROVED

Swirls and haze

Fine wash marks, dullness, and light surface defects that scatter reflections.

ASSESS FIRST

Water spots

Some marks can be reduced through polishing; severe etching may need another approach.

NO GUARANTEES

Deep scratches

Deep defects may extend beyond what is safe to polish. We assess before promising removal.

Paint correction FAQ

Before you book.

Is paint correction the same as waxing?

No. Wax adds a temporary protective layer. Paint correction is machine polishing that refines paint defects and improves clarity.

Should correction come before ceramic coating?

If the paint has defects you want improved, correction should happen before coating. A coating can leave existing marks visible beneath the protection.

Can you remove deep scratches?

Some light scratches may improve. Deep scratches may be too severe for safe polishing and need a different repair. We inspect first.
