Services in the right order

Paint correction first. Ceramic coating next. Mobile detailing to maintain the result.

Details by Dame is built around a finish-first approach. If your paint has swirls, haze, or visible defects, start by assessing whether paint correction is appropriate. Once the finish is prepared, ceramic coating can add hydrophobic protection. Mobile detailing supports ongoing interior and exterior care.

01 / RESTORE

Paint correction

Machine polishing to improve the clarity of paint affected by swirls, haze, and suitable light defects.

Explore paint correction ↗
02 / PROTECT

Ceramic coating

Hydrophobic protection applied to a clean, prepared surface. Coating does not make paint scratch-proof.

03 / MAINTAIN

Mobile detailing

Interior and exterior care at your home or workplace, subject to service-area confirmation.
